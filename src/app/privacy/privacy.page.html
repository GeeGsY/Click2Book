Privacy information

What Click2Book keeps about you, and what you can do about it.

What we keep

  • Your name, email and optional mobile number.
  • A scrambled (hashed) version of your password, never the password itself.
  • Your bookings, saved destinations, chats and notifications.
  • For each saved payment method, only its name and the last 4 digits.

Where it is kept

On this device, in the browser's storage. This prototype has no server, so your details are not sent to us or shared with anyone. Photos and fonts are loaded from the internet (Unsplash and Google Fonts), so those sites can see that the app was opened.

Your choices

  • Change your name or number under Personal information.
  • Change your password under Change password.
  • Remove saved payment methods under Manage payment methods.
  • Delete your account and everything saved for it, below.

In a full release

A real version would keep data on a secure server, check passwords there, and have its privacy policy reviewed under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act 10173).

This permanently deletes your account and bookings, and clears your chats and saved places on this device. It cannot be undone.
